Hello, I'm Elena

Permanent makeup artist in Voronezh. I help women wake up beautiful — without daily makeup routine.

My philosophy is simple: the best work is the one you don't notice. I don't do bold face tattoos. I gently enhance your natural features so people just notice you look rested and fresh.
